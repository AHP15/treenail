## R-019: "Treenail" is traditionally pronounced "trunnel", splitting the verbal brand and re-colliding with Tor's DSL

- **Severity**: low
- **Likelihood**: medium
- **Disposition**: accept
- **Date**: 2026-07-04
- **Date resolved**:
- **Description**: The woodworking term is historically pronounced /ˈtrʌnəl/ ("trunnel"/"trennel"), not "tree-nail". Anyone who knows the term and says it aloud produces a word listeners cannot spell back to the package name — and "trunnel" is exactly the Tor binary-format DSL the D-021 namespace gate rejected, so the spoken brand routes searchers to the collision the written brand avoided. D-021's gate implicitly assumed the name is only ever *read*; word-of-mouth (podcasts, conference mentions, pairing sessions) is an unexamined channel.
- **Disposition rationale**: The population that knows the traditional pronunciation is a rounding error next to readers who will naturally say "tree-nail", and the compound spelling is self-correcting in writing — the only channel that resolves to an install command.
- **Action**: Blast radius acceptable as-is; the README/guide may state «pronounced "tree-nail"» as a courtesy line when branding lands, but no guard is warranted.
- **Related decisions**: D-021
