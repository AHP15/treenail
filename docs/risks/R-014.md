## R-014: Public docs/ exposes the monetization strategy and internal planning

- **Severity**: low
- **Likelihood**: high
- **Disposition**: accept
- **Date**: 2026-07-02
- **Date resolved**:
- **Description**: D-012 publishes Keel's own decision records as a showcase, and those records (D-002, D-015) spell out the monetization model, its timing, and what is being deferred for commercial reasons.
