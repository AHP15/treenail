## R-023: Name-bearing anchors have no meaning-preserving repoint — the evidenced fact is abolished, not moved

- **Severity**: medium
- **Likelihood**: high
- **Disposition**: fix
- **Date**: 2026-07-04
- **Date resolved**:
- **Description**: D-024's repoint rule assumes every moved target has an "equivalent new path/string", but for anchors where the old name *is* the claim there is none: T-004's deliverable is the `keel-skills` package itself (`grep:"name": "keel-skills" in cli/package.json`), and after the sweep no string in the tree evidences that fact. Repointing it to `"name": "treenail"` makes the record internally contradictory — prose titled "Publish a placeholder keel-skills 0.0.x" over Evidence proving a treenail package — which is exactly the history rewriting D-024 says repointing never does. T-018 and T-020 (`npx keel-skills install`, `keel-skills update`) are the same shape. The policy covers moved targets and is silent on abolished ones, so the sweep either violates it or leaves those anchors dangling anyway.
- **Disposition rationale**: Medium × high and structurally identical to R-022 — a policy meeting a case it cannot express — so no guard closes it; the decision needed the missing rule before the sweep is decomposed.
- **Action**: Superseded by D-025 (replaces the failure mode): anchor maintenance gains an explicit abolished-target mode — pin to `git:<short-sha>:<original-anchor>`, resolvable against history — alongside D-024's repoint-when-moved. The rename sweep applies pinning to T-004, T-018, T-020 and repointing to the other four affected tasks.
- **Related decisions**: D-024, D-025
