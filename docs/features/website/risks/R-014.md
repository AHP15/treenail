## R-014: The Cloudflare-branded Turnstile widget can't be themed to the craft-meets-engineering bar and lands on the highest-craft surface

- **Severity**: medium
- **Likelihood**: high
- **Disposition**: mitigate
- **Date**: 2026-07-05
- **Date resolved**:
- **Description**: D-011 makes "craft-meets-engineering" the brand's ownable equity — a precise blueprint aesthetic, warm oak accent, structural line-work — and D-012 sets a high bar for tasteful, in-house micro-interactions. Turnstile renders as a Cloudflare-hosted widget (an iframe with a Cloudflare logo and fixed checkbox/badge chrome) whose styling is limited to a handful of preset themes and sizes; its visual language and vendor branding cannot be bent to the D-011 direction. Because it gates form submission, it necessarily sits on the contact surface — a hero-craft area — where an un-themable third-party control visibly dilutes exactly the ownable craft D-011 was minted to protect. There is no in-house alternative that keeps the bot protection, so the design bar and the protection are in direct tension D-017 did not weigh.
- **Disposition rationale**: Turnstile's chrome is a real but bounded intrusion, and Cloudflare's non-interactive mode plus theme/size presets shrink it to near-invisibility for most users. The residual after those knobs is a design-quality judgment worth applying now (mitigate) rather than a reason to drop the protection (fix); once the mitigated placement ships it can be re-triaged to accept if the residual reads as tolerable against the D-011 bar.
- **Action**: Mitigate by (1) configuring Turnstile in its managed/non-interactive mode so the interactive checkbox chrome is absent for the common case; (2) selecting the theme-matched preset (light/dark to match the dual theme of D-015) and the most compact size, and containing the widget within the form's own visual frame so residual vendor chrome is minimized; (3) placing the widget deliberately (e.g. revealed as part of the submit affordance) rather than front-and-center in the hero-craft area. Execute against the D-011/impeccable bar as part of building the widget (T-013), using the design system's theme tokens.
- **Related decisions**: D-017, D-011, D-012
