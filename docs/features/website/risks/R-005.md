## R-005: The site publishes agent-support tiers and a quickstart as public promises while the underlying product risks are unresolved

- **Severity**: high
- **Likelihood**: medium
- **Disposition**: mitigate
- **Date**: 2026-07-05
- **Date resolved**:
- **Description**: D-009 section 6 ships public "honest tiers" for Claude Code / Codex / opencode, and the quickstart (section 7) presents `npx treenail install` as a working first-run. But the retrospective flags product R-004 (interactive core degrades badly on opencode/Codex) and R-005 (no end-to-end behavioral validation on any target) as still `accept`/`high` — the real v1 exposure. The website turns those unretired product risks into a durable, indexed public claim: a Codex/opencode user who hits the degraded experience judges the product against what the landing page advertised, and there is no e2e evidence that even the Claude quickstart works end to end as shown. The marketing surface can over-promise faster than the product risk is retired.
- **Disposition rationale**: The narrative arc (D-009) is sound; the exposure is that specific claims can outrun verified product behavior. That is a content-accuracy launch gate, not a reason to drop the section — so mitigate with a source-of-truth constraint on the claims rather than fix the decision.
- **Action**: Mitigate with a pre-launch content-accuracy gate: (1) the supported-agents tier copy must be sourced from actual behavioral evidence (tie the tier wording to the product release-gate smoke / product R-004's findings, and phrase Codex/opencode to honestly convey degradation, not parity); (2) the quickstart must be run end-to-end on Claude Code and confirmed to produce the shown output before it goes live. Block launch on both. Re-check when product R-004/R-005 change disposition. Tracked by T-018.
