## T-021: Give the forms a non-Turnstile fallback path when the widget is blocked or fails to load

- **Status**: todo
- **Size**: S
- **Mitigates**: R-013
- **Depends on**: T-012, T-013
- **Acceptance**: When the Turnstile widget fails to load or render, the contact form surfaces the owner's direct `mailto:` and the newsletter form surfaces the hosted-list provider's own signup URL as an accessible fallback, and the submit control communicates why it is disabled rather than failing silently on submit.
