## D-006: Form submissions go to managed providers; the site stores zero PII itself

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-07-05
- **Context**: Adding contact and newsletter forms (D-002) means the site collects email addresses — a trust boundary and PII. Submissions could be self-persisted (Vercel Postgres/KV) or offloaded to managed providers.
- **Decision**: Offload all submissions to managed third-party providers and store no PII in our own datastore. Newsletter signups go to a hosted list provider; contact-form messages are delivered as email to the owner's inbox via a transactional email API. The site's own responsibility is limited to input validation and handing off to the provider. Specific vendor selection is a lower-leverage follow-up.
- **Rationale**: Self-persisting emails would make the site own GDPR obligations, retention policy, breach surface, and export tooling — disproportionate for an OSS project. Offloading shrinks the trust-boundary surface to "validate → hand off" and reduces the privacy commitment to a short disclosure note. Cost accepted: two third-party accounts and their API keys as Vercel env secrets.
