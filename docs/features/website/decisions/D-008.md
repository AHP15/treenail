## D-008: Landing leads with the pain, aimed at the individual developer

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-07-05
- **Context**: The hero can lead from three angles: the felt pain (agents drift/forget), the pipeline mechanics, or the artifact/records durability. The audience could be framed as individuals or teams.
- **Decision**: Lead the hero with the pain — "stop your coding agent losing the plot" — positioning Treenail as the durable planning spine that ends drift, re-litigation, and lost context; the seven-skill pipeline is presented as the "how," not the hook. Address the **individual developer** using an AI coding agent, not teams/enterprise.
- **Rationale**: The felt problem is the strongest first-contact hook; leading with the pipeline is impressive but abstract, and leading with the artifact undersells the pain. The individual-developer framing is forced by the product itself: v1 is user-level installs only (product D-008) and team divergence is explicitly deferred (product R-025), so the site must not pitch team features that don't exist.
