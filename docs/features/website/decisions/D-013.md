## D-013: Launch on a Vercel subdomain; adopt a custom domain later as a separate task

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-07-05
- **Context**: The 2026-07-04 retrospective (Watch-for #6) warns to price new public strings up front — naming has proven expensive and identity-laden for this project. A domain is exactly such a string, and no custom domain is registered yet.
- **Decision**: Launch on the Vercel-provided subdomain (`treenail.vercel.app`). Register and point a custom domain (e.g. `treenail.dev`, pending availability check) as a separate, later task once chosen — not a launch blocker.
- **Rationale**: Shipping on the subdomain avoids coupling launch to a domain-selection/purchase decision that carries the collision/pronunciation diligence the retrospective flags. Buying up front was rejected as committing a public string before it's been vetted; staying permanently on the subdomain was rejected as under-investing in the product's public identity.
