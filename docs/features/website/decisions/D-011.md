## D-011: Visual art direction is "craft-meets-engineering", executed via the impeccable skill

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-07-05
- **Context**: The name is the brand asset: a treenail (trunnel) is a wooden peg that fastens timber-frame joints with no metal — it swells to lock tight and the joint becomes the structure, mapping onto "durable planning joinery." The design could lean literal-timber, generic modern-SaaS, or a precise technical interpretation.
- **Decision**: Adopt a "craft-meets-engineering" direction: the joinery metaphor expressed through precise blueprint/technical styling rather than literal wood grain. A warm oak/amber accent on a drafting-table ground; structural line-work for the pipeline (joints/pegs as nodes); monospace for record IDs (`D-001`, `R-002`, `T-003`); generous whitespace; theme-aware in both dark and light. The `impeccable` skill drives the actual execution against this direction.
- **Rationale**: This direction is ownable — no dev-tool competitor uses timber-joinery — and ties every visual back to the name, while staying sophisticated instead of rustic. Literal timber/wood-grain risked a skeuomorphic, cozy-but-generic feel; the modern-SaaS minimal genre is safe but forfeits the name's metaphor entirely.
