## Patterns

1. **Privacy/abuse policies get specified at their happy path and are re-superseded when the first risk hits the boundary case — the project retro's Pattern 2, recurring in a new domain.** D-007 assumed a rate-limiter works in-process (R-003: not on stateless functions); D-016 assumed a hashed truncated IP isn't PII (R-010: reversible + pseudonymous-is-personal). Two supersessions in one day, each forced by the first unhandled case. The team commits to a mechanism before pricing its foundational constraint (statelessness, the legal definition of PII).

2. **A decision's cost is estimated as what it *adds*, and under-counts what a *removed* mechanism was silently guaranteeing.** Removing the IP limiter (D-016 → D-017) to satisfy zero-PII orphaned abuse control entirely → R-016 (high), re-established only by T-022. And the replacement itself re-imported the very property the removal was meant to shed: Turnstile's server-side siteverify is an unbudgeted request-path dependency (R-017) — the same shape as the R-012 KV-store objection that helped kill D-016. Mechanisms carry guarantees and costs that don't move with the requirement that named them.

3. **(Strength — preserve) `red-team-me` scoped to a fresh supersession is doing exactly its job.** D-017's own record demanded "red-team-me should be run scoped to D-017 before it ships"; that run surfaced its five residuals (R-013 blocked widget, R-014 unthemeable, R-015 undisclosed data flow, R-016 abuse cap, R-017 siteverify) *before* launch, four triaged to honest accepts and one (R-016) to a built mitigation. Paired with D-005's single-sourcing (a section-restructure absorbed for free) and T-019's one-constant URL, the front-loaded, single-source-of-truth discipline keeps late change cheap and keeps surprises at planning time. Keep it.

4. **Zero dropped tasks again (0 of 22) — reversed direction is groomed into the decision chain, not recorded as a scar.** Same after-the-fact tidiness the project retro's Pattern 3 flagged: the spam saga's two reversals are legible only through superseded decisions; the tasks built against the abandoned designs (T-012/T-013) were re-pointed silently. A zero-drop cycle with two live supersessions is a prompt to look for the redirected work, not evidence of clean aim.
