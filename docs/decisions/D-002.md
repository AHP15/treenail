## D-002: MIT license

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-07-02
- **Context**: The repo is public and adoption-driven, but the owner intends to monetize later. The license is effectively irreversible once the project has outside contributors and users.
- **Decision**: License the repo MIT.
- **Rationale**: A skills repo's whole value is being frictionless to grab; restrictive licenses (BUSL) would kill adoption. Monetization follows the ecc model — the OSS core stays free and revenue comes from a Pro tier (private repo, hosted features, support), with brand and distribution as the moat rather than the license.
