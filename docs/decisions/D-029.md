## D-029: Repurpose the degradation spike as a cross-agent parity validation doc

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-07-08
- **Context**: `docs/spike-agent-degradation.md` was slated for retirement
  because its "what degrades vs Claude Code" framing conflicts with the new
  equal-support message (D-028). But it is the only end-to-end evidence that
  all three agents run the full pipeline (challenge-me → red-team-me →
  task-brief, records auditing clean), and it is the evidence base cited for
  the two high-severity `accept` risks R-004 and R-005. The 2026-07-04
  retrospective watch-for P4 explicitly warns that parity must be *exercised,
  not asserted notionally* — retiring the doc would strip the proof at exactly
  the moment the parity claim leans on it hardest.
- **Decision**: Repurpose, do not retire. Keep the findings; reframe the
  document (retitle away from "degradation," e.g. cross-agent parity
  validation) and flip the emphasis from "what is worse on the other agents"
  to "all three complete the pipeline; only the interview surface differs."
  The reframed doc doubles as the reusable template for validating future
  agents (D-031).
- **Rationale**: The problem was never the evidence, only the framing.
  Repurposing preserves the R-004/R-005 evidence and the honest basis for the
  equal-support claim while removing the ranking connotation. Deleting it would
  make the parity claim notional — the specific failure mode the retrospective
  told us to avoid.
